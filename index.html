Privacy Policy

Local sessions — tasks, notes, and settings are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

Cloud sessions — when you create or join a cloud session, your task and note data is transmitted to and stored as unencrypted JSON on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Durable Objects). Cloud session data is automatically deleted after 24 hours of inactivity. The share code authenticates access but does not encrypt the data.

No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no accounts. Settings (clock mode, sound alerts) are always device-local and never synced.

The Export feature generates a Markdown file downloaded directly to your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
